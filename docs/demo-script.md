# PingMe — 60–90 second demo

## 0:00–0:12 — The problem

“The things we need to remember start in conversations, not todo forms. PingMe is an AI memory companion built for a friend who kept losing those small commitments.”

Show the home screen and its local-first privacy note.

## 0:12–0:32 — Brain Dump

Enter:

> Tomorrow remind me at 10 to send my resume, buy medicine in the evening, and my interview is Monday at 2. Remind me Sunday evening to prepare.

Press **Sort My Brain**.

“PingMe gives the current local date and timezone to a Gemma model, which extracts structured commitments instead of chatting.”

## 0:32–0:48 — Review

Point out the task, reminder, and event cards. Change one priority or time.

“AI never writes directly to my list. Every result is validated and I approve or edit it first.”

Press **Add to My Day**.

## 0:48–1:05 — My Day

Show the chronological timeline, stats, and generated summary. Complete one item, then open another and show edit/snooze controls.

“The commitments are stored locally in IndexedDB—there is no account and no server-side task database.”

## 1:05–1:18 — Voice and close

Press **Tell me my day** if ElevenLabs credentials are configured. Otherwise briefly show the graceful disabled state in Settings.

Open Inbox for the all-items view, then end on the home page:

**“Say it. Forget it. We’ll remember.”**

If inference is unavailable during judging, explicitly point out the “Demo parser active” badge; do not describe the fallback parser as AI.
